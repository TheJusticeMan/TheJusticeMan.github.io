# Im The Justice Man - Electronic Music Producer & Artist

From secluded farm roots to faith-fueled beats, my music is a sonic escape, rooted in curiosity, experimentation, and a drive to give life (inspired by Brennan Manning). Growing up without internet, I dissected WAV files and built simple sound apps, sparking melodies that evolved into full tracks. Now, as Justice Vellacott, I blend high-energy EDM with spiritual depth, aiming to multiply joy and point listeners to Jesus. Tracks distributed via DistroKid on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and more.

## My EDM Sound: A Sonic Blueprint

My style is electronic dance music (EDM) at its soulful core, high-energy beats layered with emotional melodies. Drawing from childhood "silly stories" with my brother Earnest, it's an escape turned legacy.

- **Melodic & Atmospheric**: Haunting synths and builds like Alan Walker’s *Aurora* or K-391’s *FLAAKLYPA*. Tracks like *Me, Myself and The Night* influence my "hooded" aesthetic, introspective yet uplifting.
- **Intensity & Hardstyle**: Raw euphoria at 150 BPM, with distorted kicks echoing Hardwell’s *Brace For Impact* or remixes like *Wellerman (Hardstyle)*. For those adrenaline rushes.
- **Metal Fusions & Nostalgia**: Symphonic twists via Megaraptor’s covers (e.g., *Blue (Da Ba Dee)*), plus 2020s trends like techno *Tetris* remixes, blending innovation with heartfelt depth.
- **Faith Thread**: Every beat carries purpose, subtly weaving in themes of hope and transformation, much like my novel _The Fall and Rise of the Mistaken Superhero_.

Tools? FL Studio Mobile, MIDI keyboard, and AI prompts via Suno (using my S.G.I.V. Formula: Style, Groove, Instrumentation, Vocals). Goal: Earworms that stick, powered by cognitive acoustics.

## My Creative Journey

Secluded childhood on a farm with siblings (shoutout to Earnest for those secret stories) built my self-taught edge, no internet, just pure tinkering. Leaving that isolation opened doors to connections, church at The River – Trumpet of Truth, and sharing music as healing.

Music isn't just art; it's agency. Like the Blog post [[Blog/Vertically Up A New Way to Fill the Earth|Vertically Up]], it's about expanding, rooted growth, not limits. From "Lawn of Green Sky Disease" silly tales to pro productions, it's all about failing forward (per my quote) to make positive impact.
